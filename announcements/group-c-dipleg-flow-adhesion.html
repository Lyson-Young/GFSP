Geldart C类颗粒在旋风分离器料腿中的流动与黏附

第一作者：杨栗昕通讯作者：陈建义

The flow and adhesion of Geldart Group C particles in the dipleg of cyclone separators

Li-Xin Yang, Jian-Yi Chen, Han-Gong Zhang, Bo Hu, Kang-Shuai Liu, Jian-Jun Zhu, Yao-Dong Wei

Chemical Engineering Journal526 (2025), 170833

摘要

In fluid catalytic cracking (FCC) units, discharge inefficiencies occur in the dipleg of the second-stage cyclone separator due to the conveyance of highly adhesive Geldart Group C particles. To address this issue, this study employs a cyclone separator (160 mm diameter) equipped with a dipleg (50 mm inner diameter, 1800 mm length). Coal fly ash, characterized as typical Geldart Group C particles (density: 733 kg/m3, median diameter: 8.46 μm), was used as the test powder. Using high-speed photography and an adhesion morphology imprinting technique, the effects of inlet gas velocity (10–25 m/s) and inlet solids concentration (0.0025–0.01 kg/m3) on the flow behavior and adhesion of fly ash within the dipleg were investigated. The results reveal that the dipleg's internal space is vertically partitioned into four zones: the Swirl Cleaning Zone, Adhesion Zone, Falling Zone, and Dense-Phase Accumulation Zone. Specifically, the starting position of the Adhesion Zone shifts downward with increasing inlet gas velocity but upward with increasing inlet concentration. The length of the Adhesion Zone increases with both parameters. Due to the competition between particle deposition flux and swirl-induced sweeping flux, the adhesion mass exhibits a hump-shaped trend with respect to both inlet gas velocity and concentration. Based on an integrated analysis combining Derjaguin-Muller-Toporov (DMT) theory and swirl energy attenuation mechanisms, a prediction model for the starting position of the Adhesion Zone was established and validated. The findings elucidate the flow and adhesion mechanisms of Group C particles in the dipleg under varying operating conditions and provide theoretical support for predicting critical adhesion regions, which is essential for mitigating dipleg clogging risks.
