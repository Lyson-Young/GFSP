Enhancing second-stage dipleg discharge in series cyclones via interstage introduction of Geldart Group A particles

Li-Xin Yang, Jian-Yi Chen, Han-Gong Zhang, Kang-Shuai Liu, Yao-Dong Wei

Journal of Environmental Chemical Engineering14(5) (2026), 124536

摘要

To address the blockage of cyclone separator diplegs caused by the adhesion of Geldart Group C particles in processes like Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC), this study proposes introducing Group A particles via an interstage inlet pipe. Bench-scale experiments identified an optimal mass ratio (MA/MC) of 10, achieving 96% anti-adhesion efficiency. This result was validated in a 1:1 pilot-scale unit, where approximately 90% efficiency was maintained. Additionally, through the entrainment of fine particles by coarse particles, the method improved the separation performance of the secondary cyclone to some extent. At MA/MC = 10, the vortex-finder outlet dust concentration was 7.5% lower than that under the baseline condition. This benefit likewise required an appropriate MA/MC: at MA/MC = 1–9, although the separation efficiency of the secondary cyclone increased, the outlet concentration remained above the baseline. This research provides a structurally simple and engineering-feasible solution for tackling the adhesion and transport challenges of Geldart Group C particles in diplegs.
