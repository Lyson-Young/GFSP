高入口气速下旋风分离器内部流场的稳定性

第一作者：杨栗昕通讯作者：陈建义

Numerical investigation on the internal flow field characteristics of a cyclone separator under highly elevated inlet velocities

Li-Xin Yang, Jing-Yi Pan, Han-Gong Zhang, Bo Hu, Jian-Yi Chen

Powder Technology484 (2026), 122903

摘要

To enhance the collection of particles below 5 μm, this study investigates intensifying the centrifugal force field via extremely high inlet gas velocities up to 50 m/s. Numerical simulations using the Reynolds Stress Model (RSM) were conducted on a PV-type cyclone separator. For the 50 m/s condition (local Ma > 0.3), compressibility effects were evaluated by coupling the energy and ideal gas state equations. Results demonstrate that across 20–50 m/s, the flow field maintains strong self-similarity and structural stability, with the “outer-vortex descending and inner-vortex ascending” core remaining robust. While compressibility introduces slight attenuation in local velocity extrema, the overall flow topology remains intact. High inlet velocities do not worsen the short-circuit flow escape ratio. Furthermore, the intense rotational kinetic energy increases “vortex rigidity,” reducing the Precessing Vortex Core (PVC) relative fluctuation area from 1.449% at 20 m/s to 0.213% at 50 m/s and effectively constraining radial swing. This study confirms the robustness of the core separation flow field under extremely high velocities, providing a theoretical foundation for the efficient separation of ultra-fine particles.
